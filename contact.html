Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

What services does Mindscope offer?

We offer comprehensive services including catering for all types of events, transport and logistics solutions, business growth and enterprise consulting, and complete event management services.

How far in advance should I book your services?

For catering and event management, we recommend booking at least 2-4 weeks in advance. For consulting services, we can often accommodate requests within a few days. Transport services can usually be arranged with 24-48 hours notice.

Do you provide services outside of Nairobi?

Yes, we provide services throughout Kenya. Additional travel charges may apply for locations outside Nairobi and surrounding areas. Contact us for specific pricing for your location.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions and special requirements?

Absolutely! We specialize in creating customized menus to accommodate various dietary restrictions including vegetarian, vegan, halal, and gluten-free options. Just let us know your requirements when booking.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, bank transfers, mobile money (M-Pesa), and major credit cards. For large events, we typically require a deposit to confirm your booking with the balance due before or on the day of service.

Do you offer emergency or last-minute services?

While we prefer advance booking for optimal service, we do our best to accommodate emergency requests when possible. Contact us immediately and we'll check our availability and capacity to help.
